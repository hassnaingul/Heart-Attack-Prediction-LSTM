How the prediction works

  1. 1Enter the patient's 24 clinical, lifestyle and environmental values.
  2. 2The server validates every field and builds a 25-feature record.
  3. 3The saved preprocessor.pkl scales and encodes the features into 48 values.
  4. 4Values are reshaped to the LSTM's (1, 1, 48) input.
  5. 5The saved lstm_model.keras returns a probability between 0 and 1.
  6. 6A score above 0.5 is classified as elevated model-indicated risk.
  7. 7The result and a downloadable report are stored in your history.
